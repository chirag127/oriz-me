### What Deloitte Looks For
1. **CPQ Technical Depth** — Can you configure products, write pricing rules, design quotes?
2. **Lead-to-Cash Process Knowledge** — Do you understand the end-to-end flow?
3. **Client-Facing Skills** — Can you explain technical concepts to business stakeholders?
4. **Agile & Delivery** — How do you work in sprints, manage timelines?
5. **AI/Innovation** — Deloitte Digital values innovation; your AI/RAG work is a differentiator

### Your Story to Tell
- "I built a hybrid-RAG search system over our CPQ knowledge base that cut onboarding time by X%"

- "I diagnose configuration and pricing issues across a large enterprise CPQ codebase daily"
- "I also build AI tooling and open-source projects that keep me fluent end-to-end"
